DealPilot for WhatsApp Web

The DealPilot sales assistant beside your WhatsApp Web chats: it drafts replies, quotes from your price list and tracks follow-ups and commission.

Download the extension (.zip)

For Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer. It is already set to use this server.

Install it (about 1 minute)

  1. Unzip closiq-extension.zip. You get a folder called closiq-extension.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (in Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the closiq-extension folder.
  4. Open web.whatsapp.com and sign in on the DealPilot panel on the right.

Keep the folder after installing: Chrome loads the extension from it. To update, download again, replace the folder and click the reload icon on the extension in chrome://extensions.
