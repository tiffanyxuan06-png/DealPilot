Privacy Notice

CashChase web app and the Closiq extension for WhatsApp Web · Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address and role (Owner or Staff) of each person who signs in.
  • Business data: the price list (including unit costs, visible to the Owner only), salespeople, commission rates, quotes, deals and the activity log.
  • Customer data that the SME's staff choose to work with: the customer's name, phone number, the WhatsApp messages of a chat the rep asks the assistant to read, quotes and their items, LHDN TIN and SSM numbers, and payment slip references the customer sends.
  • Technical data: sign-in tokens and settings stored in the browser.

We do not collect bank account numbers or card details, and we never access a bank. Slip references are stored as information only.

2. How chat data is collected

  • The Closiq extension reads a WhatsApp chat in the browser. When the newest message in the open chat is from a registered customer, it sends that chat to CashChase to draft a reply. It also sends when the rep clicks Rescan or Ask AI. Photos and PDFs are sent only when the rep clicks Quote from photo.
  • It works for customers only. A chat with someone who is not in the SME's customer list is not analysed, and nothing about it is saved.
  • Casual, non-business messages are not saved to customer memory.
  • The extension never sends a WhatsApp message by itself. It only fills in the reply box; the rep decides whether to press Send.

3. Why we use it

  • To draft replies and quotations priced from the SME's own price list.
  • To track deals, follow-up reminders, salespeople's commission and the Owner's approvals.
  • To prepare quotation PDFs and a won-orders CSV for the SME's accounting software.
  • To keep an activity log so the Owner can see who did what.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising, and we do not use the SME's data to train AI models.

4. AI processing

To draft replies, the chat text and the price list (without unit costs) are sent to an AI model: either one the SME runs on its own computer (LM Studio) or a hosted model (RunPod). The AI only writes wording. Prices, totals, discounts and commission are always calculated by CashChase's own code, and every amount in an AI reply is checked against them.

5. Who we share it with

  • Within the SME: according to the Owner's permission settings. Staff never see unit costs, margins or other salespeople's pay.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us: hosting (Replit), sign-in (Supabase) and AI (RunPod). They may only use the data to provide their service.
  • When the law requires it.

6. Transfers outside Malaysia

Some of these providers store or process data outside Malaysia [list countries, e.g. United States, Singapore]. We only use providers that protect personal data to a standard substantially similar to the PDPA.

7. How long we keep it

We keep data while the SME's account is active. When the SME closes its account or asks us to, we delete its data within [30] days, except records the law requires us to keep. Won-order records the SME exported to its accounting software are the SME's responsibility.

8. How we protect it

  • Sign-in with individual accounts, and Owner and Staff permissions checked on the server.
  • An activity log that cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the hosted service.

If a data breach is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify the affected SME and the Personal Data Protection Commissioner as the PDPA requires.
